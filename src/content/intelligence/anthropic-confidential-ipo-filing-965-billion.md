Anthropic filed a confidential draft registration statement (S-1) with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on June 1, 2026, taking the first formal step toward an initial public offering that could value the Claude-maker above $1 trillion. The filing came just four days after Anthropic closed a $65 billion Series H funding round at a $965 billion post-money valuation — nearly tripling its $380 billion valuation from just three months earlier and vaulting it past OpenAI's $852 billion valuation disclosed in March.

The numbers behind Anthropic's growth are staggering. The company's annualized revenue crossed $47 billion in May 2026, up from $9 billion in January — a 5x increase in five months. Anthropic has told investors it expects to turn a profit in the first half of 2026, a claim neither OpenAI nor SpaceX can make. The Series H was led by Altimeter Capital, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, and Sequoia Capital, with significant participation from Fidelity, T. Rowe Price, and Temasek, among others.

Anthropic is racing OpenAI and SpaceX to the public markets in what is shaping up to be the most concentrated window of high-profile tech IPOs in history. SpaceX (which merged with xAI earlier this year) is targeting a ~$1.8 trillion valuation with its public filing and roadshow. OpenAI is also preparing its own confidential prospectus. Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan are reportedly leading Anthropic's offering, with an October 2026 listing target on NASDAQ.

**Why it matters:** Anthropic's IPO will be the first major AI pure-play to test public-market appetite for generative AI at near-trillion-dollar valuations — if successful, it could unlock a wave of AI tech IPOs and permanently reshape how the capital markets value foundation model companies versus traditional SaaS and cloud businesses.
