Alibaba unveiled **Qwen 3.7 Max** at the Alibaba Cloud Summit in Hangzhou on May 20, 2026, entering the frontier model race at an Intelligence Index of 57 on Artificial Analysis — placing it in the global top 10 alongside Claude Opus 4.7 and GPT-5.5. The model scores 92.4 on GPQA Diamond, 97.1 on HMMT 2026 February (the highest in its comparison group), and 80.4 on SWE-bench Verified. It supports a 1-million-token context window and includes three reasoning modes: non-thinking, thinking, and "think max."

The headline story, however, is pricing. Qwen 3.7 Max lists at **$2.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens**, with a 90% cache discount bringing cached input to $0.25. A launch promotion through June 22 cut prices to $1.25/$3.75. By comparison, Claude Opus 4.7 costs $5/$25 and GPT-5.5 costs $5/$30 per million tokens. On a per-intelligence basis, Qwen 3.7 Max undercuts both for long-context agentic workloads by a factor of 3-10x.

The model is API-only via Alibaba Cloud Model Studio, OpenRouter, and Together AI — weights were not released. It represents a strategic bet by Alibaba to capture price-sensitive enterprise workloads moving to production, particularly in Asian markets and among cost-conscious global developers who need frontier-level capability without proprietary-model markups.

Why it matters: Qwen 3.7 Max demonstrates that the cost curve for frontier intelligence is dropping far faster than most enterprises expect — models approaching the top of the leaderboard are now available at a fraction of the price of market leaders, accelerating the commoditization of high-end AI capability.
