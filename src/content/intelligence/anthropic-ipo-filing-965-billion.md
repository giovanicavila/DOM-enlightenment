Anthropic confidentially submitted a draft S-1 registration statement to the SEC on June 1, 2026, taking the first formal step toward what could become the largest technology IPO in history. The filing comes just four days after the company closed a $65 billion Series H funding round at a $965 billion post-money valuation — surpassing OpenAI's $852 billion valuation and making Anthropic the world's most valuable private AI company.

The numbers are staggering: a $47 billion annualized revenue run-rate (up from $9 billion in January), $15 billion in hyperscaler commitments including $5 billion from Amazon, and over $50 billion in compute contracts. Anthropic's enterprise-first go-to-market strategy — selling seven- and eight-figure deals to Fortune 500 buyers for Claude, Claude Code, and Cowork — has produced revenue growth that no enterprise software company has sustained before.

Anthropic beat OpenAI to the public markets due to several structural advantages: a cleaner Public Benefit Corporation structure, faster product cadence (Claude Opus 4.8 shipped just 41 days after Opus 4.7), and fewer governance questions. The company targets an October 2026 Nasdaq listing with Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan reportedly leading the underwriting.

Why it matters: Anthropic's IPO will let public markets place a direct valuation on a frontier AI model maker for the first time, setting a precedent that will shape the entire AI investment landscape for years to come.
