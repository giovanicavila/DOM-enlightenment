Alibaba revealed **Qwen 3.7 Max** at the Alibaba Cloud Summit in Hangzhou on May 20, 2026, immediately breaking into the global top 10 with an Intelligence Index of 57 on Artificial Analysis — tied with Claude Opus 4.7, Gemini 3.1 Pro, and GPT-5.5 (medium). It posts strong scores of 92.4 on GPQA Diamond, 97.1 on HMMT 2026 February (the highest in its comparison group), and 80.4 on SWE-Verified, making it competitive with Western frontier models at a fraction of the cost.

The model supports a 1-million-token context window and is priced at $2.50/$7.50 per million tokens (input/output), with cached input at just $0.25 — a 90% cache discount. Through June 22, a 50% promotion cut effective prices to $1.25/$3.75, and new Alibaba Cloud Model Studio users receive 1 million free tokens on registration. On price-per-intelligence for long-context agentic workloads, Qwen 3.7 Max undercuts both Claude Opus 4.7 and GPT-5.5 significantly.

Qwen 3.7 Max is available via API through Alibaba Cloud Model Studio, OpenRouter, and Together AI. Weights were not released. The release continues Alibaba's rapid cadence of frontier model updates — following Qwen 3.7 Plus on June 3 and Qwen-AgentWorld on June 25 — cementing the Qwen family as one of the most active and cost-effective model lineages in the world.

**Why it matters:** Qwen 3.7 Max demonstrates that the Chinese AI ecosystem is closing the gap with US frontier labs on raw capability while maintaining a decisive pricing advantage — a dynamic that is reshaping the global AI model market and putting downward pressure on inference costs across the industry.
