Anthropic filed a confidential S-1 registration statement with the SEC in the first week of June 2026, reportedly at a valuation of **$965 billion** and approximately **$47 billion in annualized revenue**. The filing makes Anthropic the highest-valued private company in the AI industry — worth more than JPMorgan Chase, ExxonMobil, or Visa by market cap — and marks the first step toward what would be the largest AI IPO in history.

The confidential filing means the full S-1 document is not yet public, but the valuation and revenue figures have been reported by multiple financial sources. Anthropic crossed $1 billion in annualized API revenue in late 2025, and the $47 billion figure represents a roughly 47x jump driven primarily by enterprise API usage, Claude Code adoption, and the Cowork collaborative AI platform. Anthropic's decision to file before OpenAI — which has also been expected to pursue a public offering — positions the company to set the terms for how institutional investors evaluate the AI lab IPO category.

The filing comes amid a period of extraordinary financial activity for Anthropic. The company recently completed a $36 billion private credit deal arranged by Apollo Global Management and Blackstone to purchase Google TPU chips — the largest chip-financing transaction in history. Claude Opus 4.8, released in late May, reclaimed the lead on key coding and agentic benchmarks. Claude Tag, launched June 23, introduced persistent multi-user AI agents inside Slack. And the company expanded its Project Glasswing program for controlled access to Claude Mythos, its most capable (and still unreleased) model.

**Why it matters:** A near-trillion-dollar Anthropic IPO would crystallize the transition of frontier AI labs from venture-funded startups into publicly accountable infrastructure-scale enterprises — and set the benchmark for every AI company that follows it to public markets.
