President Trump signed Executive Order 14409, "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security," on June 2, 2026 — the most substantial federal AI policy action since the revocation of the Biden administration's AI safety order in early 2025. The order explicitly positions the US as the global AI leader while establishing new security frameworks for frontier AI models, directing law enforcement to prosecute criminal use of AI, and expanding the cybersecurity workforce.

The order arrives amid a flurry of AI policy activity: Florida became the first state to sue OpenAI over ChatGPT safety, Anthropic filed confidentially for its ~$900 billion IPO, and concerns about AI-driven cyberattacks outpacing human defenses continue to mount. Key provisions include collaborative private-sector partnerships to harden government information systems against AI-enabled threats, protection of American AI intellectual property from foreign exploitation, and cultivation of advanced AI-enabled capabilities for national defense.

The executive order takes a distinctly different approach from prior administration's AI policies, emphasizing innovation enablement over precautionary restrictions. It directs agencies to modernize information systems and accelerate responsible AI adoption across government while maintaining what the White House describes as an "America First cybersecurity" posture. The timing coincides with the EU AI Act's August 2, 2026 compliance deadline, positioning US policy as a competing regulatory philosophy on the global stage.

**Why it matters:** This order defines the US regulatory landscape for frontier AI development through 2026 and beyond, signaling that national security considerations — not just safety — now drive federal AI policy, with direct implications for model releases, export controls, and international AI competition.
